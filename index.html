Our approach

Helpful first. Promotional second.

Natural Health is an independent editorial-style affiliate website. We aim to explain what a product is, what it claims to do, what evidence is available, and what a buyer should check. We don't present personal stories or testimonials as verified unless we can substantiate them.

Before we recommend an offer, we check:

  • What the product contains and whether its claims are supported.
  • Whether the official seller page clearly explains price, billing, and refunds.
  • Who the product may not be suitable for and what warnings are provided.
  • Whether the affiliate relationship is disclosed near the recommendation.

Common questions

A few things worth knowing

Do you sell the products directly?

No. This is an information and affiliate website. If we feature an external offer in the future, any purchase will take place on the seller's website.

Are natural products always safe?

No. Natural does not automatically mean safe or suitable for everyone. Check ingredients, interactions, and warnings, and speak with a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.

Why aren't there any purchase buttons yet?

We're not adding unverified ClickBank offers or placeholder HopLinks. Once an offer is reviewed, we'll label its external link clearly.

Is this medical advice?

No. Our content is for general education and should not replace advice from a licensed healthcare professional.
