Before considering any nutrition program

  • Check whether the program supports regular, varied meals and an adaptable daily routine.
  • Be cautious of rigid food rules, guaranteed outcomes, or promises that sound too good to be true.
  • Look for qualified professional input, transparent sources, and clear information about who the program is intended for.
  • Read all billing, refund, and cancellation terms carefully.
